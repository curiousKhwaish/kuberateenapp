**Two real bugs hit and fixed, both worth remembering the shape of:**
1. **Script-ordering bug, not a logic bug.** The achievement modal never appeared on the first real test, even though the underlying streak/milestone math was completely correct (confirmed from the plain-text messages, which listed the right badges). Root cause: `showAchievements()` was defined in the page's closing `<script>` block (`tail`), but the *call* to it lived in the page's own body content, which renders earlier in document order — the call ran before the function existed, throwing a silent `ReferenceError`.
